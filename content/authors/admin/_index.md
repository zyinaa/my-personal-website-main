Hi, welcome to my homepage.

I am currently an M.S. student in Statistics at The George Washington University, where I conduct research under the supervision of Prof. Zhengling Qi.

My research interests lie at the intersection of statistical machine learning and artificial intelligence, with a particular focus on large language models (LLMs), self-evolving AI systems, sequential inference, and change-point detection. I am especially interested in developing statistically principled methods to improve the reliability, efficiency, and adaptability of modern AI systems.

Prior to joining GWU, I received my B.Sc. in Mathematics from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). My previous research experience spans machine learning applications in financial markets, natural language processing, and statistical modeling.

I am currently preparing to pursue a Ph.D. in Statistics, Machine Learning, or related fields.
